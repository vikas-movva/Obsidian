# Solo wedding planners pain points

- Deadlines/scheduling
  - It's not immediately clear when to do what
  - Some sort of template or guideline
  - wedding schedule
  - when to book what
- vendor shopping
  - maybe a vendor marketplace
  - comparing venue/vendor packages
  - comparing vendor prices
- after booking everything
  - planning fine details
  - run of day planning
  - day-of scheduling
- vendors all have their own invoice templates
  - better to have editable templates than a one-size-fits-all kind of solution
